# The Civilization

> The living historical archive of a simulated civilization—recording its people, decisions, wars, discoveries, disasters, economy, culture, and the consequences of time.

## The Simulation

This repository is the persistent historical record of a civilization simulation.

The player governs the civilization at the highest level. The world itself is simulated: people are born and die, institutions develop, economies change, other nations make decisions, disasters occur, discoveries happen, and consequences unfold over time.

The player does not control every individual action. Instead, they decide what the civilization needs and what major courses of action it should pursue.

## Historical Rules

1. **Time advances after decisions.** The amount of time advanced depends on the scale of the situation.
2. **The world continues to evolve.** Events may occur even when the player is deliberating.
3. **Decisions have consequences.** Success is never guaranteed.
4. **Information is imperfect.** The player receives reports and observations, not omniscient knowledge.
5. **History is persistent.** Once an event becomes established history, it is not rewritten merely because later events make it inconvenient.
6. **Statistics are snapshots.** Population, economy, military strength, resources, and other measures can change over time.
7. **People matter.** Individuals may become historically significant through leadership, discovery, conflict, achievement, or circumstance.
