# Historical Timeline

## Year 1

### Day 1 — The Beginning

The civilization's historical record begins.

No established political boundaries, institutions, major settlements, leaders, wars, discoveries, or disasters have yet been recorded.

This is the baseline from which all future history will be measured.

### Days 2–22 — The First Cultivation

The population began deliberately transforming the land surrounding the founding settlement. Workers cleared vegetation, prepared soil, and established approximately 0.6 km² of agricultural plots. Sixty-five people are now primarily assigned to farming, with the first organized fields ready for planting by Day 21.

At the same time, construction crews began replacing temporary shelter with basic permanent dwellings. By Day 20, twenty-four simple dwellings had been completed around the founding settlement. The community is beginning to resemble a permanent village rather than a temporary camp.

The work consumed significant quantities of wood, stone, and treasury resources. No formal government or territorial survey has yet been established, and the wider territory remains largely unknown.

**Result:** The civilization has secured the beginnings of a food-producing settlement and permanent housing, but its agricultural output has not yet matured enough to remove the need for careful resource management.
